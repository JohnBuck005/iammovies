## 1. Who we are
IAmoviestory ("we", "us") operates a subscription video streaming service at
[yourdomain.com]. We are the data controller for personal data collected through the service.

## 2. What we collect
- **Account data:** email address, password (hashed), display name, subscription tier.
- **Payment data:** processed by our payment provider (Paddle / Lemon Squeezy / Stripe).
  We do **not** store full card numbers. See §6.
- **Usage data:** watch history, episodes viewed, rewards/points, device type, approximate
  location (country-level for VAT/Currency), IP address (for security + geo-restriction).
- **Cookies & similar tech:** see Cookie Policy.

## 3. Why we process it (legal basis — GDPR Art. 6)
- **Contract (Art. 6(1)(b)):** to provide your account, stream episodes, process your subscription.
- **Legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)):** fraud prevention, service analytics, security.
- **Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)):** non-essential marketing emails and non-essential cookies.

## 4. Your rights (GDPR / Belgian implementation)
You have the right to: access, rectify, erase, restrict, port, and object to processing,
and to withdraw consent at any time. To exercise these, contact [privacy@yourdomain.com].
You may also lodge a complaint with the Belgian Data Protection Authority
(Autorité de Protection des Données / Gegevensbeschermingsautoriteit).

## 5. Data storage & international transfers
- Account and usage data are stored in the **EU** (Supabase, EU/Frankfurt region) to keep
  EU user data in the EU.
- Our payment processor and video CDN (Bunny Stream, EU/Slovenia) are EU-based.
- If any sub-processor transfers data outside the EEA, we rely on Standard Contractual
  Clauses (SCCs).

## 6. Payment processors
Payments are handled by a Merchant of Record (Paddle or Lemon Squeezy) or Stripe. They are
independent controllers/processors for billing data. Their privacy policies apply to
payment information. We receive only confirmation of subscription status.

## 7. Data retention
We retain account data for the life of your subscription + [90] days after closure, then
delete or anonymise. Usage logs are retained [12] months for security.

## 8. Children
The service is not directed to children under [16]. We do not knowingly collect data from
users below this age.

## 9. Changes
