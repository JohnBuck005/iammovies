## What cookies we use

## Consent management
- We show a cookie banner on first visit (EU users) with **Accept all / Reject non-essential
  / Manage preferences**.
- Non-essential cookies are set **only after** affirmative consent.
- You can change choice anytime via the "Cookie settings" link in the footer.
- We honour **Global Privacy Control (GPC)** signals where applicable.

## Analytics note
We use Microsoft Clarity (or Plausible) for product analytics — heatmaps, session counts,
aggregated click data. Clarity processes limited interaction data; it is configured to
respect consent and exclude sensitive input fields.

## Managing cookies
Most browsers let you block/delete cookies via settings. Blocking strictly-necessary
cookies will break login/playback.
